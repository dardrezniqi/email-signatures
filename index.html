On the demo brands. Pozhegu Brothers and Banka Dardania are invented companies. No real institution, product, offer, phone number, address or domain appears anywhere, and every link in the campaign email points back to this page rather than to a bank. The social glyphs and the monogram are placeholders, to be swapped for real artwork before anything ships.

Dark mode here is driven by a class so it works in any browser; in a real client it runs off prefers-color-scheme. Below 700px the examples switch to their own mobile rules automatically — the toggle is there so the same thing can be checked from a desktop.
