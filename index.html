HTML email signatures

Eight hand-coded signatures. Table layout, inline CSS, no builder and no framework anywhere. Clickable phone, email and address throughout. Use the buttons to check light/dark and desktop/mobile.

Pozhegu Brothers is an invented company used as a demo brand - the numbers, address and domain are not real. Dark mode here is driven by a class so it works in any browser; in a real client it runs off prefers-color-scheme. Social glyphs are placeholders, swap in the real brand marks before shipping.
